Logic Explanation Neural Sensing

We build learning systems whose reasoning can be written down, whose uncertainty can be trusted, and whose explanations can be checked.

A research group at CRIL, CNRS UMR 8188 · Université d'Artois, Lens, France

What we work on

Logic

Neuro-symbolic reasoning: knowledge compilation, constrained decoding, and formal guarantees for learned components.

Explanation

Concept bottleneck models, rationales, and interval-valued attributions — explanations that carry their own uncertainty.

Neural sensing

Multimodal and vision-language perception, credal and random-set uncertainty, out-of-distribution detection, medical imaging.
